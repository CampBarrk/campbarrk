In-home boarding

In-home dog boarding for NYC families who travel

Boarding at Camp Barrk is for families who travel often and want their dog cared for by someone who notices the small things. Your dog stays in a safe home environment, keeps their routine, and comes home in the same shape you left them.

We board dogs from across Brooklyn, Queens and Manhattan.

🏡 A home, not a kennel

Kennels are loud, crowded and stressful for many dogs. At Camp Barrk, boarding dogs stay in a real home with space to rest, play and settle in. In warmer months, dogs have full access to the backyard and pool, and the AC is always on.

What's included in every stay

  • Daily exercise continues. Your dog keeps their routine and fitness while you're away, with outings matched to their energy and the season.
  • Medication and special diets handled with care. Fallon is a former Licensed Veterinary Technician with 15 years of veterinary experience. She can give oral and topical medications, IM and SubQ injections, SubQ fluids, eye drops and ointments, and ear flushes, drops and ointments, and she follows feeding plans exactly.
  • Updates while you're gone. Frequent photos and texts so you can relax on your trip.
  • Small numbers. We keep groups small so every dog gets real attention.

🩺 Care for dogs with medical needs

Many boarding facilities turn away dogs who need daily medications, injections or a strict diet. We welcome them. Fallon's veterinary background means she knows what to watch for, from early signs of a stomach upset to changes in how a senior dog is moving. If something needs your vet's attention, you'll hear about it right away.

Camp Barrk is not a veterinary practice and does not replace your vet. We work alongside your vet's guidance.

Who boarding is best for

Frequent work travelers, families with recurring trips, dogs who need medications or a specific diet kept on schedule, and senior dogs and dogs with sensitive stomachs who need a calmer, more attentive stay.

Where we serve

We board dogs from Brooklyn, Queens and Manhattan, including Astoria, Long Island City, Forest Hills and Flushing. We'll work out drop-off and pickup at your meet and greet.

Boarding FAQ

Is Camp Barrk a kennel?

No. Dogs stay in a safe home environment with space to rest and play, not in kennel runs.

Can you give my dog medication?

Yes. We can give oral and topical medications, IM and SubQ injections, SubQ fluids, eye drops and ointments, and ear flushes, drops and ointments. Bring your dog's medications with clear instructions.

Will my dog still get exercise?

Yes. Daily exercise continues during every stay, so your dog keeps their routine and fitness.

How will I know how my dog is doing?

We send frequent photos and texts throughout the stay.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Multi-dog and long-stay discounts are available. Contact us for current rates.

How far ahead should I book for the holidays?

As early as possible. Holiday pricing starts one month before each holiday, and peak dates fill quickly.

What vaccinations are required?

We require Rabies, Bordetella, DA2PP, Lyme and Lepto. All dogs must also be on flea, tick and heartworm prevention before daycare or boarding.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for two days' notice before booking or cancelling. The app won't allow cancellations of daycare, boarding or home visits within 48 hours; inside that window, please contact Fallon directly.
