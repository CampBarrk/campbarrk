Dog daycare and boarding in Brooklyn

Camp Barrk gives Brooklyn dogs the exercise their energy demands, with care shaped by founder Fallon Barr's veterinary background. We serve central and southern Brooklyn, from Brooklyn Heights to Coney Island.

Built for Brooklyn dogs

A lap around the block doesn't cut it for a high-energy dog.

Camp Barrk takes Brooklyn dogs out to trails, parks and open water for real outings in small, matched groups. Your dog gets a full day of work and comes home calm, not wired.

Services for Brooklyn dogs

  • Adventure daycare: small, matched groups and real outings. High-impact adventure days Monday, Tuesday, Thursday & Friday; easygoing low-impact days Saturday & Sunday. $85/day.
  • Boarding: overnight care that keeps your dog's exercise routine going while you travel. $100/day, multi-dog discounts, long-stay from $2,500/4 weeks.
  • Medically informed care: joint-smart conditioning, aging-aware exercise and experience with sensitive stomachs.

Brooklyn FAQ

Do you pick up in my Brooklyn neighborhood?

We cover central and southern Brooklyn — see the service-area map above. If you're near the edge of the zone, reach out and we'll check whether we can fit you on a route.

Where do Brooklyn dogs go during the day?

Out of the neighborhood for real exercise — trails, open space and water for swimming — in small groups matched by size and energy.

Do you offer boarding for Brooklyn dogs?

Yes. Overnight boarding keeps your dog's exercise, feeding plan and any medications on schedule while you travel.

Is there a waitlist for Brooklyn?

Spots can fill up, especially on high-demand days. Reach out and we'll let you know current availability.
