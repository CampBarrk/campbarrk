Exercise that keeps up with your dog, at any age

Queens has plenty of dogs with big energy and long lives ahead of them. Camp Barrk plans for both.

Young dogs build stamina gradually. Older dogs keep moving with work adjusted to protect their joints. Breeds prone to knee and ACL problems get activity planned around those risks from day one.
