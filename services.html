What we offer

Daycare and boarding for dogs who need more than a nap

Camp Barrk gives active dogs in Brooklyn, Queens and Manhattan the work, adventure and rest they need — with care shaped by founder Fallon Barr's veterinary background.

Every program at Camp Barrk is built around real exercise and real recovery, so your dog comes home tired, happy and in one piece.

Most daycares aim for a worn-out dog. We aim for a dog who is stronger next month than this month.

🌲 Adventure daycare

Our daycare is built around real exercise, not a crowded room. Dogs get structured outings and pack adventures — swimming, trail running, hiking, rock scrambling and bikejoring, 8–16 miles on high-impact days — in a small group matched by size, energy and temperament.

  • Small groups. Fallon handles every group herself, with a maximum of 16 client dogs.
  • Matched packs. Dogs are grouped by energy level, play style and physical needs.
  • Built-up stamina. New dogs start slow. We increase distance and intensity gradually, the same way you'd train a person.
  • Schedule. High-impact adventure days Monday, Tuesday, Thursday & Friday; easygoing low-impact social days Saturday & Sunday. Recommended 2–3 days a week.
  • Transport. Pickup and drop-off on the Camp Barrk bus. Areas and times are set at your meet & greet.
  • Where. Daycare pickup covers Brooklyn. Queens & Manhattan families are welcome for boarding, or can meet the bus at the Grand Army Plaza stop.
Best for: high-energy and working breeds, young dogs with more energy than an apartment can handle, and dogs who do better with a job than with free play.

🏕️ Boarding

Boarding at Camp Barrk is for families who travel often and want their dog cared for by someone who notices the small things.

  • A safe home environment, not a kennel.
  • Daily exercise continues, so your dog keeps their routine and fitness while you're away.
  • Medication & special diets handled with care. As a veterinary technician, Fallon can administer oral meds, injections, SubQ fluids and more, and follow feeding plans exactly.
  • Updates while you're gone. Frequent photos and texts.
  • What to bring. Your dog's food, any medications or supplements, and an article of clothing that smells like you.
  • Pricing. Contact us for current rates — multi-dog and long-stay discounts available.
  • Peak dates book early. Holiday pricing starts one month before each holiday, so book early.
Best for: frequent work travelers, families with recurring trips, and dogs who need medications or a specific diet kept on schedule.

🩺 The care behind every program

This is what sets Camp Barrk apart. Fallon brings 15 years of veterinary experience to every dog in her care. She is a Former Licensed Veterinary Technician and is Fear Free certified in a veterinary setting.

  • Joint-smart conditioning. Some breeds are prone to luxating patellas, elbow problems or torn ACLs. We know which ones, and we build exercise around protecting those joints.
  • Aging-aware care. As dogs get older, we adjust the work to keep muscles and joints strong without pushing too far.
  • Sensitive stomachs welcome. Many of our dogs have irritable bowel and other GI issues. We follow your feeding plan exactly and know the warning signs to watch for.
  • Honest fit. If our program isn't right for your dog, we'll tell you. Your dog's well-being comes before a booking.
Camp Barrk is not a veterinary practice and does not replace your vet. We work alongside your vet's guidance.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of dogs do best at Camp Barrk?

Active, social dogs who need more exercise than city life gives them. We also welcome dogs with medical needs, as long as their vet has cleared them for activity.

How do you keep dogs from getting hurt?

We build stamina gradually, match dogs by size and energy, and adjust activity for breeds prone to joint injuries. Fallon is Fear Free certified in a veterinary setting.

Can you give my dog medication?

Yes. Fallon is a veterinary technician and can administer oral medications; eye, ear and topical medications; injections (including IM); SubQ fluids; and more. See the nursing menu above.

How much does it cost?

Contact us for current rates. Every dog starts with a free virtual intake, then an in-person meet & greet before any onsite services.

How do I get started?

Start with a free virtual intake so we can get to know you and your dog, then an in-person meet and greet before any onsite services. Fill out our interest form →
