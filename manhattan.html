Manhattan FAQ

Can I book recurring boarding around my travel schedule?

Yes — recurring and last-minute boarding both work. Share your travel schedule and we'll keep your dog's routine, feeding plan and any medications on track.

Do you pick up in Manhattan?

Daycare pickup runs in Brooklyn, but Manhattan families can meet the bus at the Grand Army Plaza stop for outing days. Boarding drop-off is arranged directly with you.

My dog has a sensitive stomach. Can you handle that?

Yes. Many Camp Barrk dogs have irritable bowel or other digestive issues. We follow your feeding plan exactly and let you know right away if something seems off.
